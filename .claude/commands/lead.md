---
description: The whole story of one lead or customer: every call, text, email and visit, appointments, test drives, trade-in offers and deals.
---

1. Run `node scripts/leads.mjs lead <code or name> --json`. If the operator named a person rather than a lead, `contact <name|mobile|email> --json` shows every lead they have had.
2. Tell the story in order, in plain sentences: how they came in, how fast we answered, what happened since, what is promised next.
3. Flag anything that should not be: a repeat enquiry we never answered, a trade-in offer about to expire, a test drive with no licence on record, finance applied without the authority.
4. End with the one next step and the command that records it.
