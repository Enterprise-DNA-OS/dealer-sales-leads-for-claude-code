# Why there is no front end

AutoPlay is a database with a subscription. Underneath, a sales lead system is ordinary: people, the car they asked about, who has them, what was said, and what happens next. What you pay for is the layer on top that lets a busy sales floor get at those records without writing a query. Pipelines, filters, notifications, a phone app.

That layer used to be the whole product, because talking to a database was hard. It is not hard any more. Open this folder in Claude Code, say "who is still waiting for a call back?" and it runs the query and answers. Ask a question the dashboard never had a report for ("which cars get asked about and never driven?", "what did Facebook cost us per sale this quarter?") and you still get an answer.

## What you gain

- **Better answers.** A dashboard shows what the vendor decided to chart. Here the sales manager asks their own question and gets it answered against their own records.
- **No per-salesperson bill.** Everyone on the floor can look. The cost does not grow with the team.
- **Your records in your database.** Plain tables. Back them up, query them from anything, leave any time.
- **Your rules.** Allocation, the answer target, what counts as quiet, how long a trade-in offer stands: settings and commands you change in plain words.

## What you give up

- **A phone app on the yard.** It runs where Claude Code runs. A salesperson on the yard texts the office or uses Claude on their phone.
- **Leads arriving on their own.** Portal and website enquiries are entered from the notification, by a person or by Claude Code reading the email. Connecting the portals and the inbox directly is part of the version Enterprise DNA builds.
- **A drag-and-drop pipeline.** Stages are a column you ask about, not cards you move.
- **A vendor help desk.** This is open source. Enterprise DNA supports the installed version for dealers that want someone to call.

## Who this fits

A dealer group whose sales manager wants answers more than screens, and whose office already lives in email and a DMS. If the floor needs a live pipeline on a screen all day, Enterprise DNA builds that screen over the same records.
